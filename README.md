
 Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis Dashboard

 1.Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis 
An interactive Power BI dashboard and SQL‑Python workflow analyzing **3,900 retail transactions** to uncover spending patterns, customer segments, product preferences, and subscription behavior.


 2.Purpose  
This project simulates a real-world business analytics workflow**, transforming raw customer transaction data into actionable insights. The dashboard helps businesses understand who their customers are, what they buy, how they shop, and what drives loyalty.


 3. Tech Stack  
- 🐍 Python (Pandas, NumPy) – Data cleaning, feature engineering, EDA  
- 🗄️ PostgreSQL – Business queries, segmentation, revenue analysis  
- 📊 Power BI – Interactive dashboards and KPI visualization  
- 📝 Data Modeling – Age groups, purchase frequency, subscription segmentation  
- 📁 File Formats – `.ipynb` for notebooks, `.sql` for queries, `.pbix` for dashboards, `.pdf/.pptx` for reports  


 4. Data Source  
- Synthetic dataset of 3,900 purchases across demographics, product categories, discounts, shipping types, and subscription status.  
- Features include: Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status, Purchase Amount, Discounts, Shipping Type, Review Ratings.  


 5. Features 
 • Business Problem  
Retailers struggle to identify loyal customers, discount dependencies, and subscription drivers without structured analytics.  

 • Goal of the Dashboard  
To deliver an interactive tool that:  
- Segments customers into New, Returning, and Loyal.  
- Compares subscriber vs. non‑subscriber spending.  
- Highlights top products, discount dependency, and shipping preferences.  
- Provides age group and gender‑based revenue insights.  

 • Walkthrough of Key Visuals  
- KPIs (Top Left): Avg spend $59.76 · Avg rating 3.75 · Subscribers 27% · Non‑Subscribers 73%  
- Revenue by Gender: Male customers generated ~158K USD vs. 75K USD for female customers.  
- Subscription Breakdown: Avg spend nearly identical, but non‑subscribers drove higher total revenue.  
- Top Products:Gloves (3.86⭐), Sandals (3.84⭐), Boots (3.82⭐).  
- Customer Segmentation: Loyal (3,116), Returning (701), New (83).  
- Shipping Insights:Express shipping users spend ~$2 more per transaction.  

 • Business Impact & Insights  
- 📈 Boost Subscriptions: Target repeat buyers who are not yet subscribers.  
- 🎯 Customer Loyalty Programs: Reward loyal customers to increase retention.  
- 💰 Discount Policy Review: Balance margin control with sales boosts.  
- 🛍️ Product Positioning: Highlight top‑rated products in campaigns.  
- 👥 Targeted Marketing: Focus on high‑revenue age groups and express‑shipping users.
